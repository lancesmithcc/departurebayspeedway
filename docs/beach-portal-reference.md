# Snuneymuxw beach portal

Local reconstruction of the missing two-pole red metal arch visible at 04:36 and 04:40 in the supplied video frames. It stands seaward of the beachfront bend opposite the 7-Eleven/Bay Street area. Approximate world placement (-694, -1065); coordinate and carving proportions are visual estimates, not surveyed measurements.

Reference: https://nanaimonewsnow.com/2018/06/21/its-like-taking-back-what-was-ours-sfn-totem-poles-installed-at-departure-bay-beach/

The report identifies Joel Good as the carver, dates unveiling to June 2018, gives pole height as 16 feet, and states the carved faces point toward the sea. Its photograph supplements the road-facing backs visible in the user's stills. This is distinct from the earlier three-post Portal to Our Heritage at Kin Park.

Rebuilt geometry includes two cedar posts, carved relief faces and feather panels, a red metal silhouette with actual cutouts, base footings and hardware. Painted faces point seaward; the road sees the wooden backs. Carving is an approximation of visible forms, not a facsimile or scan. No additional cultural meanings are asserted.

Ground uses rendered terrain contact; posts and narrow metal legs join the building collision grid, leaving the opening passable. Nine merged material meshes.
