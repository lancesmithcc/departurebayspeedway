# Power-up visuals and police bar combat

Blessed riders retain their authored model with a warm emissive glow and a halo above their head. Touching nearby pedestrians turns copies of their current character shapes into bright silhouettes that accelerate upward, shed light particles and fade. Dense groups are processed together. Ascended characters leave collisions until a new run, without triggering Jesus/Satan death hooks. Shared character materials and geometry are preserved.

Lucky beer blends decaying half-resolution HDR frame history with subtle blur. Double-double increases saturation and bloom. Post effects run only while riding with the matching pickup; replacements, expiry, state changes and resize invalidate history.

Nanaimo bars can aim at and defeat pursuing officers. Swept contact consumes each bar once, stops officer fire immediately and plays the recorded warning: “The law will catch up with you soon, you little punk!” The officer falls onto the rendered surface. Encounter cleanup still follows expiry, vehicle generation changes and reset.

Validation: `npm run check:power-effects`, voice, hazard, multiplayer and character lifecycle checks. Beer and coffee shaders and blessing/ascension were visually checked in a local WebGL browser. These effects are local player simulation, consistent with existing NPC combat; this change does not synchronize NPC events between multiplayer clients.
