# Departure Bay Speedway
Browser dirtbike game inspired by Departure Bay Road, Nanaimo, from Country Club to the beach. Local residents and friends play as an exaggerated fictional dirtbiking character with Nanaimo bars. Up to seven named, coloured riders share a room; traffic and scoring remain client-local. Marketing must distinguish game screenshots from illustrated campaign art and must not imply official City affiliation or real-world driving instruction.
## Platform
Web: static HTML, CSS, JavaScript and Three.js; existing Node server.
## Brand commitments
User requests Road Rage and Work Sans, opening-screen-style action art, Nanaimo slang, responsive animated promotional pages, truthful place schema and discoverable local topics. Preserve the game's entry route.
