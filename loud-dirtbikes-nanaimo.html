A few things, bud.

How can I deal with loud dirtbikes in Nanaimo?

For real-world noise concerns, start with the City of Nanaimo’s noise information and complaint options. This game cannot receive or resolve complaints.

Can I play without the engine noise?

Yes. Press M to mute game audio. The waiting-room beat sound is optional and starts switched off.

Is this an official Nanaimo simulator?

No, bud. Departure Bay Speedway is an independent fictional arcade game inspired by the local road and neighbourhood.

Can I play with other Nanaimo residents?

The shared room supports seven active players. Each enters a display name and gets a different colour; extra players wait for a spot.
