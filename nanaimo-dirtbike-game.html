A few things, bud.

Is Departure Bay Speedway free?

Yep, bud. Play in your browser. No download or account required—enter a rider name and join.

Is this an exact road or driving simulator?

It is a stylised arcade game based on Departure Bay Road. We use street references and terrain data, but scenery and physics are adapted for play and are still being refined.

How do I ride?

Use W or the up arrow to accelerate, S to brake, A/D to steer, Space to jump and F to throw a Nanaimo bar. Touch controls appear on supported phones.

Can my friends see me?

Yes. Up to seven active riders share the room, with names and individual colours. Traffic, pedestrians and scoring run locally for each player.
