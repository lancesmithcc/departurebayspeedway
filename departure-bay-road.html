Where is Departure Bay Road?

Departure Bay Road is in Nanaimo on Vancouver Island, British Columbia. The game depicts the run from the Country Club area toward Departure Bay Beach.

Does the artwork show the exact road?

The large hero illustrations are fictional campaign art. Images captioned as game captures show the current simulation. Neither is a navigation guide.

What makes it Nanaimo?

The setting, the hills, the coastal finish, the Nanaimo bars and a cast with plenty of “holy crap, bud” energy. The game is local satire, not a claim about particular residents.
